# Stories

## Reinforce troops

As a player 
I want to select number of troops and place them in my territories
 Sothat I can defend my territory and attack opponent territories.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Given** I am a player
- **When** I click on my territory
- **Then** I am able to select number of troops and place them in my territory.

- [ ] **Given** I am a player
- **When** I click on opponents territories
- **Then** My troops will not be placed there.

- [ ] **Given** I am a player
- **When** I place troops
- **Then** I have to know about remaining number of troops.

- [ ] **Given** I am a player
- **When** I am out of troops
- **Then** I should deligate to next phase.

## Attacking

As an attacker 
I want to attack neighbour territories. 
So that I can increase my territories.

- [ ] **Given** I am a attacker
- **When** I click on my territory
- **Then** I am able to select it.

- [ ] **Given** I am a attacker
- **When** I select my territory
- **Then** I can see neighbour territories that I can attack.

- [ ] **Given** I have already selected a territory
- **When** I select an another territory of mine
- **Then** last selected territory should be selected for attack.

- [ ] **Given** I have selected my territory
- **When** I click on my neighbour territory
- **Then** I am able to choose number of troops to attack

- [ ] **Given** I have choosen troops to attack from my territory
- **When** I click on attack
- **Then** dice will roll.

- [ ] **Given** I am a attacker
- **When** I select my territory and neighbour's territory
- **Then** I am able to cancel the attack

## defending

my territory
